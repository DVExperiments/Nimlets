Keep it like an app

Your Nimlet lives in this browser, on this device. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon.

iPhone and iPad
In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Do this before you hatch your egg, then always play from the icon: a pet started in Safari stays in Safari. Safari may also clear a website's saved data after about a week away; the home-screen app keeps it.
Android
In Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. A pet you've already started comes with you.
Computer
Chrome and Edge offer to install it from the address bar. Otherwise, bookmark this page.

Clearing this site's data in your browser settings clears your pet too.

What it's like

Visits are short: feed it, play a quick game, clean up, turn the lights off at bedtime. It never dies of neglect. When it's grown, it leaves an egg behind and the next generation begins in the same room, with the old drawings still on the wall.

Underneath, it's a small artificial-life experiment. It learns your habits, invents little signals to ask for things, and sometimes guesses what you'll do next. It only notices what happens inside the game: no camera, no microphone, no account.
